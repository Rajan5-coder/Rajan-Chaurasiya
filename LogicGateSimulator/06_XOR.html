ABOUT GATE

The XOR gate gives output 1 when the inputs A and B are different. If both inputs are the same, the output is 0.

A B Y(A⊕B)

BOOLEAN EXPRESSION

Y = (A⊕B)

1 = HIGH(ON)

0 = LOW(OFF)
